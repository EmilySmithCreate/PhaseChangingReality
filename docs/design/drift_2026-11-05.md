## Current definitions (from `HYPOTHESIS.md`; unchanged here)

- A square holds one real c in [−1, 1]: + is curl on the black diagonal (matter), − on the white (antimatter). A
  full curl carries a half twist of its sign; a store is a flag meaning two halves joined into one full twist.
- A pair is made full, (+1, −1), on the two squares sharing a link. A pair cancels into light where there is room
  (an empty square beside either); same-sign neighbours with no room join into a store. Room is judged once on the
  made state for every pair at the same instant (her clock). A store cannot move and cannot cancel.
- The store's price comes from gravity, one-directional, never spent (a one-way counter in the toy).
- **New (5 November):** the halves drift under gravity before they join.

## Her prediction (owed, in her words, before the first registered run)

What stores per pair does with the drift against without it, as λ rises and as R grows; whether the stores gather
into one cluster; and whether like hands neighbour each other without a repulsion rule. The one-in-a-billion is not
reachable on these sizes (a boundary row's share is 1/R, so a billionth needs R of order 10⁹).

## What this build can and cannot show

It can show whether a drift gathers the halves, whether the gathering raises or lowers the store count, and what
share of the leftover halves sits on the cluster's edge. It cannot show the billionth, the poles without a rule for
them, or anything about the sky.

## Proposed form of the force between halves (6 November 2026, night; ours from her words, hers to accept)

The pull is gravity's, with the sign and shape of gravity: negative, zero at infinite separation, and on the
two-dimensional sheet logarithmic in the separation (the solution of Poisson's equation in two dimensions),
which rises without limit and so never lets the halves come free, the confining shape without a separate
't Hooft rule. The push is the repulsion of like-handed twists near contact, positive (the DNA note's K_0 form is
the published shape). The store is locked on the repulsive side by the loop closing, and its extra mass, the
proton's worth, is the repulsion energy stored; gravity's negative account is paid by the infalling energy. If
she accepts this, choices 1 and 3 above are set and 't Hooft's linear form is withdrawn from the drift.
