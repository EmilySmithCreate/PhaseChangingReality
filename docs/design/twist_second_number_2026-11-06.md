# Design brief: twist as its own number on the squares (6 November 2026, night)

Her reversal of 6 November, night, of her decision of 4 November: "b) make twist its own number again". Written
before any code (rule 20); the choices below have our plainest defaults and are hers to accept or change; the build
follows the defaults and is marked so. Nothing here is a result.

## Current definitions (from `HYPOTHESIS.md` and the moves module; unchanged here)

- **The state** (3 November): one real number c in [−1, 1] per square; c > 0 is curl on the square's black
  diagonal (matter), c < 0 anti-curl on its white diagonal (antimatter); only |c| = 1 is a stable store of energy.
- **The moves** (3 and 4 November; `graphity.curl_pair_moves`): a pair is made full, +1 and −1 on two squares
  sharing a link, costing two units of the release; where there is room (an empty square beside either) a +1 and
  a −1 sharing a link cancel into light, both squares emptied, two units leaving; where there is none, two same-sign
  curls sharing a link join into a Klein store, a flag on one square with the partner emptied; room is judged once
  on the made state (her clock); the store's price comes from gravity, a one-way counter; her kind rule for which
  side of a boundary takes which kind (T107).
- **Twist, until tonight**: not a second number; a full curl carries a half twist of its sign (4 November).
- **Her decisions of 6 November that this brief carries**: twist is its own number again; a store is one loop over
  two diagonals (n = 2, τ = 1, L = 2√2); two opposite half twists that cancel into light leave their curls behind
  as a dark gluon (curl with no twist, the neutral pion's shape); the gravity counter is the work of the drift,
  locked at the join as the store's mass (not built here; the flat price per join stays as its stand-in).

## The object: one state, two numbers per square

Per square: **c**, the curl as now; **t**, the twist in halves, t in {−1, −½, 0, +½, +1}; and a store mark joining
two squares. A free half: |c| = 1, t = ±½ with the sign of c. A dark gluon: |c| = 1, t = 0. A store: two squares
sharing a link, both with |c| = 1 and the same sign, marked as one store, t = ±1 on the store (±½ on each square).
Empty: c = 0, t = 0.

## The choices (defaults ours; hers to accept or change)

| # | Choice | Plainest default (ours) | What it decides |

| 1 | **What leaves as light when twists cancel.** | A full curl's one unit is a count part and a twist part; the twist part is a share s of the unit, **s = ½** (a half twist is half the unit), a stand-in until the twist-energies reader gives κ. ## What the build can and cannot show

It can hold her dark gluon and count it; it can keep every registered result unchanged (choice 3); it can give
the twist-energies reader a store that is two squares. It cannot show what a gluon weighs (that needs s from κ, or
κ from the proton's calibration of sigma), the billion, or anything about the sky.

## Her prediction (owed, in her words, once the build exists)

The number of dark gluons against stores at the freeze on a sheet, as λ rises and at the held-out size.
